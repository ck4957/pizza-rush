# Market Analysis

Research date: 2026-07-28 UTC

## Target segment

General-audience iPhone players who enjoy 30–90 second cooking, arcade, and
time-management sessions with one-thumb controls, visible mastery, and local
progression.

## Demand signals

- **Good Pizza, Great Pizza** is free with in-app purchases, has more than
  300,000 US ratings, a 4.7 rating, and an Editors' Choice designation. This is
  strong evidence for durable pizza-making demand:
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/good-pizza-great-pizza/id911121200
- **Cooking Fever** offers thousands of dishes and more than 1,000 levels,
  showing scale and retention demand for upgrade-driven restaurant play:
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cooking-fever-restaurant-game/id714796093
- **Cooking Craze** remains a maintained free restaurant game spanning many
  cuisines:
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cooking-craze-restaurant-game/id1029094059
- The directly named **Pizza Rush - Idle Game** has reviews complaining about
  back-to-back ads. This supports a differentiating ad policy built around
  opt-in rewards and bounded natural-transition interstitials:
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pizza-rush-idle-game/id6639609772

## Competitors and substitutes

1. Good Pizza, Great Pizza — deeper narrative pizza-business simulation.
2. Cooking Fever — broad online restaurant progression.
3. Cooking Craze — broad multi-cuisine time management.
4. Pizza Rush - Idle Game — directly named idle production-line substitute.
5. Pizza Rush! — stacking runner with a near-identical title.

## Differentiation

Pizza Rush centers a visible, capacity-limited conveyor rather than disconnected
mini-games or an idle worker loop. Preparation, oven timing, cutting, and
dispatch occupy physical slots, so one-thumb drag decisions create spatial
planning and recoverable pressure. Deterministic gameplay, offline level
progress, non-color timing cues, and strict ad caps support a higher-trust
experience.

## Distribution

Use App Store keywords around pizza, cooking, restaurant, arcade, casual,
time-management, baking, kitchen, and one-thumb play. Product screenshots should
show the conveyor, perfect oven timing, lunch-rush overlap, upgrades, and
results. GitHub Pages provides support, privacy, terms, and marketing URLs.

## Risks

- The fixed name has direct App Store collisions.
- The genre has strong incumbents and expensive content expectations.
- Twenty levels, six recipes, upgrades, Game Center, advertising, and purchase
  compliance create a substantial version-one scope.
- Ad-heavy competitors have trained users to distrust monetization.
- Art consistency, level balance, and oldest-device performance require
  runtime proof beyond a passing build.
