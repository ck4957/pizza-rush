# Pricing and Monetization Decision

Research date: 2026-07-28 UTC

## Decision

Use a **freemium hybrid** model:

- Free App Store download.
- Optional user-initiated rewarded ad to double level coins after a completed
  level, once per completed level.
- Limited interstitial after every three completed non-tutorial levels with a
  minimum 180-second cooldown, never after rewarded advertising, never during
  gameplay, and never immediately after launch.
- One non-consumable **Remove Ads** purchase at a planned US price of **$4.99**.
  It disables interstitials; rewarded ads remain optional.
- No subscription, consumable currency sale, paywall, account, or RevenueCat.

## Validation

Good Pizza, Great Pizza validates a free entry point and optional purchases.
Pizza Rush - Idle Game currently lists a $4.99 No Ads purchase, providing a
direct category comparator. Its low-rating complaints about repeated ads support
the conservative cap. Price remains subject to App Store Connect tier mapping
and current storefront availability.

## Value metric

Completed level sessions, durable World 1 progression, and voluntary boosts
without a progression wall.
