# Color Palette and Contrast

Calculated WCAG contrast ratios:

| Foreground | Background | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `#332C29` charcoal | `#FFF2D9` warm cream | 12.36:1 | Primary text |
| `#66733D` olive | `#FFF2D9` warm cream | 4.64:1 | Secondary text and status |
| `#4D7594` ceramic blue | `#FFF2D9` warm cream | 4.42:1 | Large controls only |
| `#332C29` charcoal | `#F1B942` success gold | 7.67:1 | Success badges |
| `#332C29` charcoal | `#D99B52` baked crust | 5.71:1 | Filled control labels |
| `#D94A32` tomato | `#FFF2D9` warm cream | 3.81:1 | Large icons/fills, not body text |

Tomato red is never used as small text on cream. Controls use charcoal labels
or icons and shape boundaries. Oven readiness also uses dot, checkmark, star,
exclamation, and smoke symbols so meaning never depends on color alone. Dark
mode uses warm cream text on charcoal and preserves the same semantic roles.
