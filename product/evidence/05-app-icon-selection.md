# App Icon Selection

Two independent opaque square ImageGen concepts were generated.

| Concept | 60 px silhouette | Pizza/oven meaning | Motion | Palette fit | App Store crop | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Oven Slide | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 25/25 |
| Perfect Burst | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 23/25 |

**Oven Slide** is selected. Its diagonal pizza, visible oven arch, single cheese
strand, and restrained streak read as one action without relying on text or a
timing symbol. The 1254 × 1254 RGB master is opaque; a flattened 1024 × 1024 RGB
derivative is retained for the Xcode asset catalog.
