# Product Design Selection

Exactly three independent ImageGen gameplay directions were generated from the
approved 390 × 844 mobile brief.

| Direction | Journey | Readability | Accessibility | Platform fit | Feasibility | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Warm Kitchen Conveyor | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 25/25 |
| Countertop Sprint | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 21/25 |
| Four-Stage Storybook | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 21/25 |

**Warm Kitchen Conveyor** is selected. It is the only direction that makes all
four stations, the left-to-right capacity order, the bake window, the current
recipe, and thumb-reachable ingredients simultaneously legible without turning
the screen into a dashboard. Its shapes map cleanly to SpriteKit nodes and a
SwiftUI HUD.

The other directions remain evidence, not implementation targets.
