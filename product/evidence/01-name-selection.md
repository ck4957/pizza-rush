# Name Selection

Research date: 2026-07-28 UTC

## Decision

The selected and owner-fixed name is **Pizza Rush**. Alternatives were evaluated
only to preserve collision evidence; they are not authorization to rename:

1. Pizza Rush — selected because it is the fixed product identity, concise,
   pronounceable, Siri-friendly, and directly describes the pressure fantasy.
2. Ovenline Rush — more distinctive and describes the conveyor, but less
   immediately about pizza.
3. Slice Shift — compact and rhythmic, but could imply a puzzle rather than a
   cooking game.
4. Brick Oven Dash — descriptive, but longer and less ownable as a product
   family.

## Current collision evidence

- An exact-near name, **Pizza Rush!**, is already visible in the US App Store:
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pizza-rush/id1591016899
- **Pizza Rush - Idle Game** is also active in the US App Store:
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pizza-rush-idle-game/id6639609772
- Additional related names include **KB Pizza Rush** and **Epic Pizza Rush:
  Pizza Games**:
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kb-pizza-rush/id6479623892
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/epic-pizza-rush-pizza-games/id6757471024
- Multiple Google Play games use Pizza Rush, including package
  `com.ninecircle.pizzarush`:
  https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ninecircle.pizzarush
- A WHOIS query returned no current match for `pizzarushgame.com` at the
  research timestamp. That is a point-in-time observation, not a reservation.
- Search-engine checks did not establish reliable availability for matching
  major social handles.

## Risk

The fixed name has a high App Store collision risk and may be rejected by App
Store Connect as unavailable. Public search and a preliminary trademark search
are not legal clearance. No trademark ownership or freedom-to-operate claim is
made. The implementation must preserve the fixed name until App Store Connect
or the owner authorizes a change.
