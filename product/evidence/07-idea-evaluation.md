# Idea Evaluation

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem severity | 3 | The need is entertainment rather than high-stakes, but short-session pressure and mastery are clear. |
| Audience clarity | 4 | Casual cooking and time-management players are a specific, proven audience. |
| Market demand | 4 | Large maintained incumbents and direct substitutes demonstrate demand. |
| Differentiation | 4 | The capacity-limited physical conveyor is meaningfully different from idle and disconnected mini-game loops. |
| Monetization fit | 4 | Free entry, optional reward, bounded interstitials, and ad removal match the genre without subscription pressure. |
| Distribution fit | 3 | App Store discovery and visual screenshots are plausible, but the fixed name collision weakens search identity. |
| Delivery feasibility | 3 | Native 2D implementation is feasible, while twenty levels and compliance make the first release substantial. |
| Design coherence | 5 | Conveyor, palette, icon, gestures, animation, audio, and progression reinforce the same fast pizzeria fantasy. |

Arithmetic mean: **3.75**

Decision: **go**. Every score is at least 3 and the mean exceeds 3.5. The fixed
name collision and runtime/content scope remain explicit release risks.
