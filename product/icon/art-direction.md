# Pizza Rush App Icon Art Direction

The icon must communicate one pizza, one oven, and one fast action at home-screen
size. It uses an opaque square master of at least 1024 × 1024 pixels.

## Selected composition

A pepperoni pizza slides diagonally out of a compact brick oven toward the
viewer. One cheese strand and one restrained speed streak imply motion. The
pizza and oven arch create one strong silhouette; no text, characters, coins,
full kitchen, tiny ingredient details, or embedded rounded-square mask are
allowed.

## Generated concepts

- `option-1-oven-slide.png`: diagonal oven-to-pizza movement on a tomato-red
  field.
- `option-2-perfect-burst.png`: front-on perfect-bake burst framed by a dark
  oven.

`option-1-oven-slide.png` is selected because the oven remains recognizable,
the action reads without the star cue, and the red background gives stronger
small-size separation. `selected-master.png` preserves the original 1254-pixel
RGB master and `app-icon-1024.png` is the flattened 1024-pixel asset-catalog
source.
