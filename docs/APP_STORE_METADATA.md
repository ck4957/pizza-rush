Description:

> Turn a cozy corner pizzeria into the fastest kitchen in town.
>
> Pizza Rush is a hands-on arcade cooking game built around one readable production line. Start the dough, add the exact ingredients, catch the perfect bake window, slice the pie, and dispatch it before your customers lose patience.
>
> MASTER THE KITCHEN
>
> Every pizza moves through four visible stages: Preparation, Oven, Cutting, and Dispatch. Capacity is limited, so the challenge is deciding what to start next and keeping every station moving.
>
> CHASE THE PERFECT BAKE
>
> Watch the oven carefully. Deliver a pizza undercooked or overcooked and your reward drops. Hit the perfect window to earn a bonus, build your combo, and reach three-star scores.
>
> GROW YOUR PIZZERIA
>
> Play 20 handcrafted Corner Pizzeria levels, including five challenge shifts. Unlock six recipes and spend earned coins on practical upgrades for preparation, baking, cutting, delivery, and ingredients.
>
> PLAY YOUR WAY
>
> Assist Mode provides larger targets, longer customer patience, a wider perfect-bake window, and slower order pressure without reducing progression or rewards. Bake state uses text and symbols in addition to color, and Reduce Motion is respected.
>
> FAIR, OPTIONAL MONETIZATION
>
> Pizza Rush has no account and no progression paywall. Optional rewarded ads can double one completed level's coins after a verified reward. Limited interstitials appear only at eligible level transitions and can be removed with a one-time purchase.
>
> Can you keep the whole kitchen moving when the lunch rush arrives?
