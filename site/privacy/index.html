Privacy Policy

Pizza Rush has no account, custom server, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK. Game progress is stored locally on your device. Advertising services may process limited data as described below.

What Pizza Rush stores

Coins, scores, stars, unlocked levels, upgrades, settings, tutorial steps, ad-reward claims, and cached Remove Ads entitlement state are stored locally using Apple frameworks. Pizza Rush does not upload this game profile to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and consent

Pizza Rush uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your region and device configuration, Google may process coarse location inferred from IP address, device identifiers, product interaction, advertising data, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for third-party advertising, analytics, app functionality, and fraud prevention.

Pizza Rush requests non-personalized/contextual advertising, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not authorize cross-app tracking. UMP is used to collect or present privacy choices where required.

Apple services

Apple processes Remove Ads purchases through StoreKit and may provide Game Center authentication and scores. Pizza Rush receives verified entitlement or game-service state, not payment-card information. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's policies.

What we do not collect

Pizza Rush does not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone content, health or fitness data, financial details, messages, user-generated content, browsing history, or search history.

Children

Pizza Rush is a general-audience game intended for ages 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not designated Made for Kids.

Data choices

When required, open Settings → About & Support → Advertising Privacy Choices. You can also limit device-level ad measurement through iOS settings. Deleting the app removes its local game profile; Apple purchase history remains with your Apple Account.

Contact

For a privacy or support question, open a support issue. Do not post personal, account, or payment information in a public issue.

Changes

If the shipped app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the affected release.
