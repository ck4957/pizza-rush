Help from the pizzeria

Support

If something is getting between you and the perfect bake, start here.

Report a bug

Open a public support issue and include your iPhone model, iOS version, Pizza Rush version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not include personal or payment information.

Open a support issue

Restore Remove Ads

  1. Open Pizza Rush.
  2. Choose Settings, then About & Support.
  3. Choose Restore Purchases.

Restore uses your Apple Account purchase history. Pizza Rush never receives your payment-card details.

Ads or privacy choices

Pizza Rush requests non-personalized/contextual advertising and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. When Google requires a privacy-options entry, it appears under Settings → About & Support → Advertising Privacy Choices.

Reset local progress

Settings → Reset Progress removes local coins, stars, upgrades, and tutorial progress. A Remove Ads purchase remains restorable through Apple.

Accessibility

Assist Mode provides larger targets, longer patience, a wider perfect-bake window, and slower order pressure without reducing rewards. See the accessibility notes.
